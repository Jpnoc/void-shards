## Full description (max 4000)
```
The void is hungry. Feed it nothing.

Void Shards is a dark, neon match-3 puzzle game built for players who want a real challenge. Swap glowing shards, forge devastating specials and set off chain reactions, but the board fights back.

⚡ A MATCH-3 THAT FIGHTS BACK
• Void Bombs count down with every move. Let one reach zero and the sector is lost.
• Corruption spreads to a new tile every turn you fail to destroy some of it.
• Obsidian takes two hits, frost cracks layer by layer, chains lock shards in place, and holes reshape how shards fall.
• Tight move limits and six shard types from sector 4 onward. Every swap matters.

💥 DEVASTATING SPECIALS & COMBOS
• Match 4 to forge a Beam Shard that clears a whole line.
• Match in an L or T to forge a Nova Shard that blasts a 3×3 area.
• Match 5 to forge a Prism that wipes out every shard of one colour.
• Swap two specials together for Crossfire, Supernova and Total Erasure.

🌌 INTENSE VISUALS & SOUND
• Glowing gems, shockwaves, screen shake and particle storms.
• A live, generated soundtrack that speeds up as the danger rises.
• Haptic feedback on big explosions.

🏆 20 BRUTAL SECTORS
• Boss sectors every five levels throw every threat at you at once.
• Earn up to three stars per sector and chase high scores.

💎 BOOSTERS & DAILY REWARDS
• Earn Void Crystals by winning, with bonuses for stars.
• Claim a daily reward that grows with your streak.
• Use Hammer, Shuffle and Defuse boosters when a sector gets tough.
• Out of moves? Watch an optional ad for +5 moves.

Every sector can be beaten without paying. Ads are always optional.

Can you seal the void?
```
