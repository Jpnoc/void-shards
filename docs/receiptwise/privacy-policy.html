ReceiptWise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 November 2026

In short: your receipts are stored only on your phone. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to an AI service so it can be read. We do not have user accounts, we do not sell data, and we show no ads.

What the app does with your data

  • Receipts you scan or type in (store, date, items, prices, categories, returns, budgets) are saved on your device only. We cannot see them.
  • Receipt photos. When you scan, the photo is sent to our server, which forwards it to an AI model (Google Gemini) to extract the text and sort the items. Our server does not store the photo or the result. The photo may contain personal information printed on the receipt (for example a partial card number or a name); please avoid photographing anything you do not want read.
  • Optional photo thumbnail. If "Keep a small photo of each receipt" is on in Settings, a small copy is stored on your phone only. You can turn this off.

The AI service

We use the free tier of the Google Gemini API. Under Google's terms for that tier, content sent to it may be used by Google to improve its products, and may be reviewed by people. If you prefer not to share a receipt this way, use "Enter manually", which sends nothing. See Google's Gemini API terms.

Servers and logs

Requests pass through a Cloudflare Worker that we operate. Cloudflare may process your IP address to deliver the service and prevent abuse (rate limiting). We do not keep our own logs of receipt content.

What we do not collect

No name, email, phone number, location, contacts, advertising ID, or analytics. The app has no accounts and no ads.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Export your data any time (Settings, Export CSV).
  • Delete all receipts in Settings, or clear the app's storage or uninstall to remove everything from your device.
  • Because we hold no copy of your receipts, there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, notify you in the app.
