**Full description:**
```
Stop shoeboxing receipts. ReceiptWise turns a quick photo into a tidy budget.

📸 SNAP IT
Take a photo of any sales receipt. AI reads the store, date, every item and the tax in seconds.

🗂️ SORTED AUTOMATICALLY
Each item lands in a neat category: Groceries, Dining, Transport, Health, Household, Clothing, Electronics, Entertainment, Personal Care and Other. Not right? Change any item with one tap.

💰 TOTALS THAT UPDATE THEMSELVES
Every time you add a receipt you instantly see your total for today, this week, this month and this year.

↩️ RETURNED SOMETHING?
Tick "Returned" on an item and its price, plus its share of tax, comes straight off your totals and budgets.

🎯 MONTHLY BUDGETS
Set a limit per category and watch the progress bars. See at a glance where you are over.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads. Receipts are stored only on your device. Export everything to CSV any time.

No camera? Pick a photo from your gallery, or type a receipt in by hand.
```
