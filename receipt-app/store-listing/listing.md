**Full description:**
```
Stop shoeboxing receipts. ReceiptWise turns a quick photo into a tidy budget, and keeps your business expenses and mileage ready for your accountant.

📸 SCAN IT
Take a photo of any sales receipt, or import a PDF invoice. AI reads the store, date, every item and the tax in seconds.

🗂️ SORTED AUTOMATICALLY
Each item lands in a neat category: Groceries, Dining, Transport, Health, Household, Clothing, Electronics, Entertainment, Personal Care and Other. Not right? Change any item with one tap.

💰 TOTALS THAT UPDATE THEMSELVES
Every time you add a receipt you instantly see your total for today, this week, this month and this year.

↩️ RETURNED SOMETHING?
Tick "Returned" on an item and its price, plus its share of tax, comes straight off your totals and budgets.

💼 BUSINESS OR PERSONAL
Tag each receipt, add notes, and filter in one tap. Export a CSV report of business expenses and mileage for any period.

🚗 MILEAGE LOG
Log business trips with from, to, distance and purpose. Set your rate per mile or km and see the value add up.

🎯 MONTHLY BUDGETS
Set a limit per category and watch the progress bars. See at a glance where you are over.

🔒 YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads. Receipts and trips are stored only on your device. Photos and PDFs are sent securely to an AI service only to be read.

No camera? Pick a photo from your gallery, or type a receipt in by hand.
```
